# 11 Incubator

Human-curated observations, candidate improvements, experiments, and proposed patches. The
incubator cannot apply, commit, certify, or promote its own output.

An incubating operational candidate may be named as lineage when a human or trusted compiler
imports the matching Git definition. Linked targets use the exact `Kind:slug@version` form. The
registry verifies the candidate is incubating and that all three target coordinates match, then
stores a set-once repository-import link and audit event. That provenance does not certify,
release, execute, apply, or promote the candidate.
