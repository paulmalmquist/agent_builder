# Console grammar

Paul OS is an instrument for resolving exceptions, not a feed to consume.

## Attention

- Only decisions create badges.
- Safety stops share the degraded shelf and carry an explicit red label.
- Informational events wait for the next successful daily briefing.
- A cleared queue says **All quiet** and shows the last delivered briefing time.

## Progressive disclosure

- L0 is one status mark.
- L1 is a verdict-first headline and a change delta.
- L2 adds cost, authority, one primary action, one secondary action, and provenance.
- L3 contains the full instrument view, evidence, and raw technical detail.

## Language

The first two lines answer: what is this, what happened, and what should the reviewer do. Use
short active sentences. Explain platform terms in ordinary language. Every action names its
consequence and its undo. Every risk statement says what the capability cannot do.

## Interaction

`J` and `K` move through review items. `A` and `R` open governed approve or reject dialogs.
`E` opens detail. Shortcuts do nothing inside inputs, text areas, selects, editable regions, or
dialogs. A keyboard shortcut never performs a mutation by itself.

## Visual hierarchy

Purple means a decision is needed. Amber means degraded and always includes an icon and label.
Red is reserved for an explicit safety stop. Nominal state is quiet. Motion uses only transform,
opacity, or shadow and must honor reduced-motion preferences.
